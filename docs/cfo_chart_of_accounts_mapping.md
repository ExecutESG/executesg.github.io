## Overview

Modern sustainability reporting starts in the general ledger. By mapping standard European Chart of Accounts (DATEV SKR03/SKR04, Nordic BAS, Finnish tilikartta) directly to GHG Protocol categories and **VS (VSME)** disclosures, CFOs and accounting firms eliminate manual spreadsheet data collection.

## General Ledger Mapping Architecture

1. **4000–4999 Materials & Subcontracting:** Maps to Scope 3 Category 1 (Purchased Goods & Services) via [Invoice Line-Item vs. Spend-Based Carbon Accounting](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/invoice-line-item-carbon-accounting-vs-spend-based).
2. **5000–5999 Personnel & Workforce:** Feeds VS (VSME) Social disclosures (headcount, training hours, safety).
3. **6000–6999 Facility Power, Heating & Fleet Fuel:** Maps directly to Scope 1 fuel liters and Scope 2 utility kWh.
