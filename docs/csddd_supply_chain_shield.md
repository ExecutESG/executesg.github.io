## Overview

Under the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) and CSRD trickle-down mandates, enterprise buyers must verify environmental and labor compliance across their tier-1 and tier-2 industrial suppliers. The **VS (VSME)** voluntary standard acts as the legal value-chain cap: enterprise buyers cannot demand bespoke data points beyond the VS (VSME) framework from SME suppliers.

## Key Compliance Modules

1. **Environmental Baseline (B1–B3):** Verified Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions plus waste diversion.
2. **Workforce & Safety Baseline (B8–B11):** Accident rates (LTIFR), living wage compliance, and gender pay metrics.
3. **Governance & Anti-Corruption (B12):** Code of conduct and supply chain due diligence policies.
