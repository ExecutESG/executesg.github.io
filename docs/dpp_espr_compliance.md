## Overview

The European Union Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) mandates Digital Product Passports (DPPs) across batteries, textiles, iron and steel, aluminum, chemicals, and industrial components. For small and mid-sized European manufacturers, the EFRAG **VS (VSME)** standard provides the standardized operational baseline (Disclosures B1, B2, and B3) required to populate buyer DPP requests.

## Core Computational Metrics

1. **Cradle-to-Gate Carbon Footprint (`kg CO2e / unit`):** Aggregates Scope 1 process fuel, Scope 2 electricity ([Location-Based vs. Market-Based](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/scope-1-2-3-emissions)), and upstream raw material inputs.
2. **Circular Material Content (`% Recycled Input`):** Maps directly to VS (VSME) Disclosure B3 circular economy metrics.
3. **Value-Chain Cap Protection:** Aligns supplier disclosures with the [EU Omnibus & VS (VSME) Value-Chain Cap](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/eu-omnibus-vsme-value-chain-cap).
