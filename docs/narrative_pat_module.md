The **Narrative-PAT (Policies, Actions, and Targets) Module** is the strategic governance module of the European **VS (VSME)** standard. It enables enterprises with established sustainability practices to disclose their governance models, transition roadmaps, and stakeholder alignment.

Maintained and authored by the **ExecutESG Editorial Team** ([https://executesg.com](https://executesg.com)).

---

## 🎯 The Three Pillars of Narrative Disclosure

### 1. Policies (Strategic Commitments)
* How executive leadership integrates ESG into commercial business decisions.
* Aligning corporate strategy with stakeholder interests: [Shared Understanding and Leadership Alignment Playbook](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/shared-understanding-leadership-playbook).
* Fostering psychological ownership: [Psychological Ownership and Corporate Sustainability](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/psychological-ownership-corporate-sustainability-sdt).

### 2. Actions (Operational Execution)
* Concrete decarbonization, circularity, and social initiatives deployed in the current fiscal year.
* Overcoming the strategy execution gap: [Filling the Strategy Execution Gap in Sustainability](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/filling-the-strategy-execution-gap-sustainability).
* Actionable leadership frameworks: [Sustainable Decision-Making Framework for Corporate Leadership](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/sustainable-decision-making-framework-leadership).

### 3. Targets (Quantifiable Trajectories)
* Time-bound targets for greenhouse gas reduction, energy efficiency, and waste elimination.
* Science-based targets alignment: [SBTi Target Setting for VS (VSME) Reporting](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/sbti-targets-vsme).
* Continuous progress monitoring: [Modern Stakeholder Engagement & Materiality](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/modern-stakeholder-engagement-double-materiality).

## 🧠 Double Materiality Assessment (DMA) Integration

Under the VS (VSME) framework, SMEs can conduct a streamlined Double Materiality Assessment to identify their material Impacts, Risks, and Opportunities (IROs):

* **Impact Materiality:** Inside-out assessment of corporate operations on the environment and society.
* **Financial Materiality:** Outside-in assessment of climate risks and regulatory changes impacting balance sheets.
* Read the comprehensive tutorial: [Impact vs Financial Materiality: Core Differences](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/impact-vs-financial-materiality-difference).
* Master the methodology: [Ultimate Guide to Double Materiality for European Businesses](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/ultimate-guide-double-materiality).
* Eliminate survey fatigue: [Stop Survey Fatigue: Pairwise Comparison Alternatives](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/stop-survey-fatigue-pairwise-alternatives).
* Mathematical rigor: [Pairwise Comparison DMA Complete Guide](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/pairwise-comparison-dma-complete-guide).
* Flaws of Likert scales: [Why Likert Surveys Fail in Materiality Assessments](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/why-likert-surveys-fail-materiality).
* Delphi vs MLE models: [MLE Consensus vs Delphi Method for Stakeholder Consensus](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/mle-consensus-vs-delphi-method).
