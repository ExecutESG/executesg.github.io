The **Basic Module** is the foundational tier of the official European Voluntary Standard for Non-Listed SMEs—designated as **VS (VSME)**—issued by the European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG). It is specifically tailored for micro, small, and medium enterprises requiring a straightforward, proportionate disclosure mechanism.

Maintained and curated by the **ExecutESG Editorial Team** ([https://executesg.com](https://executesg.com)).

---

## 📋 Core Metrics & Indicator Architecture

The Basic Module covers twelve standardized disclosure requirements across Environmental, Social, and Governance pillars:

### 1. Environmental Disclosures (B1 – B4)
* **B1: Energy Consumption & Energy Mix**
  * Total energy consumption measured in megawatt-hours (MWh).
  * Breakdown of fossil fuels (diesel, natural gas, heating oil, petrol) vs. renewable energy share.
  * Practical guide: [Energy and Fuel Tracking Protocols for European SMEs](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/vsme-metrics-prioritization-guide).
* **B2: Greenhouse Gas Emissions (Scope 1 & Scope 2)**
  * Direct emissions from owned or controlled operations (Scope 1).
  * Location-based and market-based indirect emissions from purchased electricity and heating (Scope 2).
  * Step-by-step calculation: [Scope 1 & Scope 2 Emissions Guide for European SMEs](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/scope-1-2-3-emissions).
  * Comparative analysis: [Scope 1 vs Scope 3 Emissions: Where SMEs Should Begin](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/scope-1-vs-scope-3-emissions).
* **B3: Water Resources & Withdrawal**
  * Total freshwater withdrawal in cubic meters ($m^3$).
  * Identification of operations in water-stressed European river basins.
  * Environmental deep-dive: [Nature Footprint & Handprint Guide for Businesses](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/nature-footprint-and-handprint-guide).
* **B4: Waste Generation & Circular Economy**
  * Tonnage of non-hazardous waste directed to recycling vs. landfill.
  * Hazardous waste tracking and regulatory compliance records.

---

### 2. Social Disclosures (B5 – B9)
* **B5: Workforce Demographics & Headcount**
  * Total headcount and Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) distribution.
  * Temporary, permanent, and seasonal employee breakdowns.
* **B6: Gender Diversity & Remuneration**
  * Percentage of female employees across executive management and total workforce.
  * Unadjusted gender pay gap calculation methodology.
* **B7: Health, Safety & Occupational Well-being**
  * Lost-time injury frequency rates (LTIFR) and workplace incidents.
  * Psychosocial safety frameworks: [ISO 45003 Psychosocial Risk Assessment Guide](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/iso-45003-psychosocial-risk-assessment-guide).
  * Employee well-being models: [Job Demands-Resources Model in Sustainability](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/job-demands-resources-model-workplace-wellbeing).
* **B8: Training & Skills Development**
  * Average training hours per employee per annum.
  * Future of work analysis: [Sustainability Skills and the Future of Work for SMEs](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/sustainability-skills-future-of-work).
* **B9: Collective Bargaining & Employee Representation**
  * Percentage of workforce covered by collective bargaining agreements.

---

### 3. Governance Disclosures (B10 – B12)
* **B10: Anti-Corruption & Business Ethics**
  * Written code of conduct and anti-bribery policies.
* **B11: Whistleblower Protection**
  * Secure reporting channels compliant with EU Whistleblower Directive 2019/1937.
* **B12: Regulatory Convictions & Fines**
  * Monetary fines and legal penalties associated with environmental or labor violations.
