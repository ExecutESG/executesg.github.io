## 🔬 GHG Accounting Methodologies

### 1. Scope 1: Direct Operations Emissions
* Fuel combustion in stationary equipment (natural gas, heating oil, diesel generators).
* Mobile combustion in corporate vehicle fleets.
* Fugitive emissions from air conditioning and refrigeration refrigerants.
* Full methodology: [Scope 1 vs Scope 3 Emissions Guide](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/scope-1-vs-scope-3-emissions).

### 2. Scope 2: Purchased Electricity & Energy
* **Location-Based Method:** Calculated using national grid average emission factors (DEFRA / EEA / IEA).
* **Market-Based Method:** Reflecting contractual arrangements, Guarantees of Origin (GoOs), and green power purchase agreements (PPAs).
* Exhaustive guide: [Scope 1, 2, 3 Emissions Master Guide for European SMEs](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/scope-1-2-3-emissions).

### 3. Scope 3: Value Chain Emissions
* Purchased goods and services (Category 1).
* Capital goods (Category 2).
* Upstream transportation and logistics (Category 4).
* Business travel and employee commuting (Categories 6 & 7).
* Detailed walkthrough: [How to Disclose CDP Scope 1, 2, 3 Emissions](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/how-to-disclose-cdp-scope-1-2-3-emissions).
