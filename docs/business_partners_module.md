The **Business Partners Module** of the European **VS (VSME)** standard provides standardized data points specifically required by corporate value chain partners, commercial banks, tender boards, and rating agencies (such as EcoVadis, CDP, and Sedex).

Authored and maintained by the **ExecutESG Editorial Team** ([https://executesg.com](https://executesg.com)).

## 🛡️ The "EcoVadis Tax" & Supplier Fatigue Relief

SMEs across Europe face an avalanche of uncoordinated sustainability questionnaires from corporate buyers. The VS (VSME) standard serves as the official European "Shield" capping value chain requests.

* Understand the burden: [The EcoVadis Tax: Overcoming Supplier Fatigue](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/the-ecovadis-tax-supplier-fatigue).
* Prepare your disclosures: [VS (VSME) Preparation for EcoVadis Audits](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/vsme-ecovadis-preparation).
* EU regulatory protections: [EU Omnibus Directive & VS (VSME) Value Chain Caps](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/eu-omnibus-vsme-value-chain-cap).
* German supply chain law: [Lieferkettengesetz SME Supplier Relief](https://executesg.com/resources/blog/lieferkettengesetz-sme-supplier-relief).
